# Login artwork

The desktop and mobile backgrounds were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The login form is real HTML, separate from the artwork. The dashboard keeps its operational layout.

- [Desktop background](../app/static/brand/gatherspace-login-desktop.png): badminton in the lower-left foreground, then table tennis, basketball and a gym; group interaction at lower right, with login space above it.
- [Mobile background](../app/static/brand/gatherspace-login-mobile.png): a separate portrait composition with the login form below the sports scene.
- Styling and responsive selection: `app/static/gatherspace.css`.

## Desktop generation prompt

```text
Edit the supplied GatherSpace login illustration, preserving its premium soft 3D architectural miniature style, pale icy-blue background, teal infrastructure paths, warm oak and muted amber accents, realistic human proportions, gentle daylight and clean polished rendering. Create a WIDE 16:9 desktop login background, preferably 1920x1080. Recompose the whole scene for real login UI overlays, not a screenshot. No lettering, logos, numbers, watermark or drawn login form.

Composition: the upper-left 40% width and top 17% height are quiet blank background reserved for the product title. The upper-right rectangle x=73%–98%, y=8%–57% must be completely EMPTY pale background for a real login form. Do not place any people, chat cards, bot, or architecture behind this reserved rectangle. Put the group-and-robot island entirely in the LOWER RIGHT x=73%–96%, y=65%–94%, unobstructed and readable: one friendly small white assistant robot, two seated people, two simple upright chat cards with blank graphical lines. A compact venue scheduling hub with a calendar panel is at lower center x=49%–68%, y=64%–88%, with teal paths connecting all venues and the group island.

Replace the left venue collection with exactly FOUR recognizable sports miniatures in a flowing ascending sequence: (1) BADMINTON is the largest, nearest foreground scene in the BOTTOM LEFT x=4%–43%, y=48%–92%. A real long rectangular turquoise badminton court, correctly drawn singles/doubles sidelines and service lines, ONE single net across the center width, four clearly separated adult doubles players, two per side, each holding exactly ONE small badminton racket in only one hand. Natural balanced ready stances, two arms and two legs each, no overhead action poses; one tiny shuttle. Badminton is the primary focal point. (2) TABLE TENNIS behind it at middle left x=5%–25%, y=27%–43%, exactly one rectangular table with one low center net and two adult players at opposite ends each with one small solid paddle. (3) BASKETBALL behind near upper center-left x=28%–47%, y=23%–44%, a clearly marked small half-court with a single hoop/backboard at its baseline, two players, one basketball only. (4) GYM upper-middle x=51%–68%, y=23%–45%, a wood-floored gym corner with one stationary bike and neatly racked dumbbells, one person riding the bike in a normal seated posture. All court planes, equipment scale, nets and activities must make physical sense. Keep islands apart, don’t merge courts or equipment. Use sparse, anatomically clean people rather than crowds. One racket per racket player, no duplicate limbs, no impossible hand grips. Leave generous breathing room between these modules. The reference is a style and concept source; remove the craft room, yoga room and second group island.
```

## Mobile generation prompt

```text
Create a separate PORTRAIT 9:16 mobile login background for GatherSpace, around 1080x1920. Use the supplied wide illustration ONLY as a style/content reference. Keep the same premium soft 3D miniature architecture, icy pale-blue seamless background, teal paths, warm oak, restrained amber, friendly small robot and realistic human anatomy. This is a mobile website background, not a phone mockup. No text, logos, watermark, buttons or drawn login form.

Very deliberate overlay zones: top 0%–14% is EMPTY pale background for a real title. Bottom 66%–100% is EMPTY pale background for a real mobile login form. Confine ALL illustrated objects to the middle 17%–62% height, within x=5%–95%. Keep a few percent of clear breathing room above the login zone. Compose one dense but orderly miniature campus flowing vertically, readable at phone width. Largest foreground focal point is BADMINTON at middle-lower left (approximately x=7%–68%, y=39%–60%), real rectangular turquoise court, correct badminton doubles/singles/service lines, just one net across its width, four adult players split two per side; each has exactly two arms/two legs and holds just ONE badminton racket with only one hand, in natural grounded ready stances. No jumping or awkward action, no merged limbs. Behind/above it, show TABLE TENNIS at x=8%–42%, y=21%–33% with one correct rectangular table/net and two players at opposite ends with single solid paddles, BASKETBALL half-court at x=48%–88%, y=18%–33% with one hoop/backboard and two players sharing one ball. A small GYM at x=56%–89%, y=34%–42% has a stationary bike with a rider seated normally and neatly stored dumbbells. Exactly four separate sporting areas, physically plausible court layouts and equipment.

A SMALL friendly robot-and-group island is at lower right x=71%–94%, y=48%–60%, with one robot and two seated adults and a single stylized upright chat card; this must stay ABOVE the blank form area. A compact calendar board between sports modules represents shared scheduling. Connect modules using restrained teal walkways. Avoid crowds and clutter, make hands/equipment anatomically coherent, no extra arms, no repeated rackets in a player's hands. All top and bottom reserved UI space stays completely empty; do not put any architecture below 64% image height.
```
